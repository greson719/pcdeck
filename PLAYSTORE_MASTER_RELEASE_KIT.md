### 📄 Full Description (Formatted for Play Store Search & Conversion)
```text
Transform your Android smartphone into a wireless multi-touch trackpad, desktop screen mirror, and remote controller for your Windows PC. PCDeck works 100% offline over your local Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot—no cloud accounts, no internet required, and zero telemetry.

Connect in seconds by scanning the pairing QR code displayed on your PC screen, and take control of your desktop from across the room.

KEY FEATURES:

🖱️ Multi-Touch Precision Trackpad
• Smooth, responsive mouse cursor movement with inertia.
• Tap gestures: 1-finger tap for Left Click, 2-finger tap for Right Click.

• Drag-and-drop toggle for moving windows and files easily.

🖥️ Desktop Screen Mirroring
• Stream your Windows desktop directly to your mobile screen.
• Switch between 30 FPS and smooth 60 FPS streaming modes.
• Direct touch interaction: tap, click, and navigate on your mirrored PC desktop.

⌨️ Live Keyboard & Shortcuts
• Type on your mobile keyboard and send text directly to active Windows apps.
• Slide-up quick typing input bar.
• Quick-action shortcut hotkeys for Windows navigation and desktop control.

📁 Local Wi-Fi File Sharing
• Browse PC Desktop and Downloads folders directly from your phone.
• Upload photos, videos, and documents from phone to PC.
• Download files from PC to phone storage over high-speed local Wi-Fi without cables.

🎵 PC Audio Streaming & Media Deck
• Stream PC audio directly to your phone earphones over Wi-Fi.
• Media playback deck: Play, Pause, Next, Previous, and Windows Volume controls.
• Live audio visualizer wave display.

⚡ 3-Second Instant QR Pairing
• Point your phone camera at the PC server window to connect automatically.
• Zero manual IP typing required.
• Works on home Wi-Fi networks and direct Mobile Hotspots.

100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE:
PCDeck establishes a direct peer-to-peer connection between your phone and your computer over your local network. Your data never leaves your local network.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
• Windows PC running the lightweight PCDeck companion server (Windows 10 / 11).
• Android device connected to the same Wi-Fi network or PC Mobile Hotspot.
```

### Step 4: Mandatory 14-Day Closed Testing Track Setup (Google Play Policy Requirement)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Mandatory Closed Testing Policy**:
> For all personal Google Play developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, Google enforces a mandatory policy: apps **cannot** be published directly to Production without first completing **Closed Testing with at least 12 opted-in testers continuously over 14 days**. Attempting to skip to Production will result in an immediate submission block.

Follow these exact steps to set up your Closed Testing track:

#### 4A. Create the Closed Testing Track
1. In Google Play Console, go to **Testing > Closed testing** (in the left navigation).
2. Click **Create track** (or select the default **Closed testing — Alpha** track).
3. Track name: `Closed Testing` (or `Alpha`).

#### 4B. Set Up the Tester List (12+ Testers)
1. Click on the **Testers** tab for your closed testing track.
2. Under **How testers join your test**, select **Email lists**.
3. Click **Create email list**, enter a list name (e.g. `PCDeck Testers`), and add the email addresses of **at least 12 to 20 testers** (friends, colleagues, or alternate Google accounts).
4. Save the email list and select it for this track.
5. *(Alternative: Create a Google Group, add your testers, and enter the Google Group address).*

#### 4C. Create the Closed Testing Release
1. In the Closed testing track, click **Create new release**.
2. Under **App bundles**, upload:
   📂 `c:\Users\GRESON\Documents\mobile_tracpad_for_pc\playstore_assets\PCDeck.aab`
3. **Release name:** `2.7.0`
4. **Release notes (en-US):**
```text
PCDeck v2.7.0 Closed Testing Release:
• Ultra-responsive wireless multi-touch trackpad with ballistic acceleration and tap clicks
• Real-time 60 FPS desktop screen mirroring with direct touch control and kinetic inertia
• Full virtual mechanical keyboard, Function keys (F1-F12), and Windows hotkeys
• High-speed local Wi-Fi file sharing between phone and PC with zero cloud dependency
• Real-time stereo PC audio streaming to phone earphones
• 3-second instant QR code pairing over local network
```
5. Click **Next > Save > Review release > Start rollout to Closed testing**.

#### 4D. Distribute Opt-In Links to All 12+ Testers
1. Go to **Testing > Closed testing > Testers** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom to find **How testers join your test**.
3. Copy both:
   - **Join on Android link**: (Opens Google Play app directly on tester devices)
   - **Join on the web link**: (Opens browser opt-in confirmation page)
4. Send these links to all 12+ testers.
5. **CRITICAL REQUIREMENT**: Each tester MUST click the link, click **Become a tester**, and install/keep the app installed on their device.
6. Ensure all 12+ testers remain actively opted in for **14 consecutive calendar days**. Track your progress on the Play Console Dashboard under **"Closed testing"**.

#### 4E. Apply for Production Access
1. After 14 continuous days with 12+ opted-in testers, the **"Apply for production"** button unlocks on your Play Console Dashboard.
2. Fill out the brief verification questionnaire:
   - How did you recruit testers? (e.g., "Invited team members, colleagues, and beta community users via email").
   - What feedback did you receive? (e.g., "Testers validated touch responsiveness, screen mirroring frame rates, and Wi-Fi pairing reliability across various Android devices").
   - What changes did you make? (e.g., "Fine-tuned trackpad deceleration curves and Wi-Fi buffer pacing").
3. Submit the questionnaire. Google typically reviews and approves production access within 2 to 7 days.

#### 4F. Roll Out to Production
1. Once Production access is approved, navigate to **Release > Production**.
2. Click **Create new release** (or promote the existing verified build from Closed testing).
3. Confirm bundle `PCDeck.aab` (v2.7.0, Code 270) and release notes.
4. Click **Review release > Start rollout to Production**.

## 🔄 4. How to Update the App in the Future

When you add new features, fix bugs, or release an update:

### Step 1: Increment Version Number
Open `package.json` or `config.xml` and increase the version code:
- **Version Name**: e.g., `2.7.1` (or `2.8.0`)
- **Version Code**: e.g., `271` (must always increment by +1 for Google Play: `270` -> `271` -> `272`)

### Step 2: Build the New Signed `.aab`
Run the build script in PowerShell:
```powershell
python build_android.py
```
*(Or if using Cordova/Gradle):*
```powershell
cd platforms/android
./gradlew bundleRelease
```
Copy the newly built `.aab` to `playstore_assets/PCDeck.aab`.

### Step 3: Update Showcase Graphics (If Features Changed)
If you updated the UI or added new slides, regenerate the 8 showcase graphics anytime with:
```powershell
python tools/build_final_grounded_showcase.py
```
All updated 1920×1080 and 1024×500 PNG files are automatically exported into `playstore_assets/`.

### Step 4: Upload the Update in Google Play Console
1. Go to **Release > Production**.
2. Click **Create new release**.
3. Upload the new `playstore_assets/PCDeck.aab`.
4. Enter the new version number (`2.7.1`) and release notes describing what changed.
5. Click **Review release > Start rollout to Production**.
