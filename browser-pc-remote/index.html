Control Your PC From Your Phone Browser Without Installing an App

Need to control a PC from an iPhone, iPad, Mac, Chromebook, or friend's device without installing software from an app store? PCDeck includes a built-in zero-install web controller accessible directly over local Wi-Fi.

Updated 1 September 2026 · Works in Safari, Chrome, Edge, Firefox · iOS, iPadOS, Android, Mac & Linux

Direct Answer

Yes. You can control a Windows or Linux PC from any smartphone or tablet browser without downloading an app. When PCDeck runs on your PC, it serves a lightweight HTML5 touch controller over your local Wi-Fi network. Scanning the PC's on-screen QR code instantly opens a responsive multi-touch trackpad, virtual keyboard, live screen viewer, and audio stream in mobile Safari, Chrome, or Firefox.

Zero-Install Architecture

PCDeck embeds a self-contained local web server. Pointing any modern mobile browser to your PC's local IP address provides full remote control with zero cloud logins, zero tracking, and zero app store downloads.

Why use a browser-based PC remote?

While native mobile apps offer background services and hardware volume button hooks, browser remote control offers distinct advantages in everyday scenarios:

  • iOS & iPadOS Support without App Store Friction: Apple devices can immediately act as wireless trackpads, keyboards, and secondary screens in mobile Safari without needing an Apple ID or TestFlight install.
  • Guest & Family Access: Let friends, colleagues, or family members control a living-room media PC or presentation without asking them to download software.
  • Temporary Emergency Access: When your mouse or keyboard dies, you don't need to waste time installing an app on your phone—just scan the QR code and start clicking immediately.
  • Cross-Platform Compatibility: Works across Android, iPhone, iPad, macOS, ChromeOS, Linux laptops, and Windows tablets.

Step-by-step setup guide

  1. Launch PCDeck on your host computer

    Run PCDeck on Windows (via Microsoft Store or portable .exe) or launch PCDeck on Linux to generate your optical pairing QR code.

  2. Scan QR code with your phone camera

    Open your phone camera (iPhone, iPad, Android, or tablet) and point it at the QR code on your PC monitor.

  3. Open Web Remote in browser

    Tap the link in your camera app to launch the PCDeck Web Remote in Safari, Chrome, or Firefox. No app store installation or registration is required.

  4. Control your computer immediately

    Touch the trackpad surface to move the PC cursor, type on the keyboard, view the live desktop screen, or stream audio to your earbuds.

⚡ 30-Second First-Time Connection Checklist

  • Same Wi-Fi Network: Ensure your phone is connected to your local Wi-Fi router (not mobile cellular data 4G/5G).
  • Windows Defender Firewall: When launching PCDeck for the first time, click Allow access on Private networks so local socket packets can flow.
  • Turn Off Active VPNs: If either your PC or phone is running an active commercial VPN (NordVPN, ExpressVPN, etc.), pause it temporarily so local subnet traffic isn't routed through external tunnels.

How the zero-install web remote works under the hood

PCDeck runs a lightweight HTTP and WebSocket server on port 8000. When your phone browser requests the page, the PC delivers the static touch deck assets. Once loaded, the browser establishes a direct WebSocket connection back to the PC over your local Wi-Fi network.

Touch coordinate deltas and keystrokes are sent over this local socket in binary packets, while screen frames are streamed and painted onto an HTML5 Canvas. Because all communication stays strictly on your local subnet, the connection is immediate, private, and works even when your internet connection is completely offline.

Home Screen Shortcut Tip

In mobile Safari or Chrome, tap Share > Add to Home Screen (or the browser menu's Add to Home screen). This creates a standalone, full-screen icon on your phone that launches PCDeck like a native application without browser URL bars.
