Why stream PC sound to your phone?

There are several common situations where using your phone as a wireless audio receiver solves immediate hardware problems:

  • Broken 3.5mm audio jack: If your laptop's headphone socket is loose, static-filled, or dead.
  • No Bluetooth on desktop: Custom desktop PCs often lack Bluetooth antennas, making wireless headphones unusable without dongles.
  • Late-night movie watching: Watch Netflix, YouTube, or gaming sessions from bed without waking anyone up.
  • Longer range than Bluetooth: Wi-Fi streams through walls across your entire house where Bluetooth cuts out after a few meters.

How to Stream Audio to Your Phone with PCDeck

PCDeck sends your computer's sound directly to your phone over your home Wi-Fi, without needing any extra cables, dongles, or complicated setup.

  1. Open PCDeck on your Windows PC

    Start the PCDeck program on your computer. It automatically picks up your computer's audio.

  2. Connect your phone

    Make sure your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi (or your phone's hotspot). Scan the QR code to connect.

  3. Plug your headphones into your phone

    Sound from your PC will play through your phone's headphones in clear stereo with no lag.
