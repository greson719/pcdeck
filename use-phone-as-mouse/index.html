How to Use an Android Phone as a Mouse for Windows

Whether your physical mouse battery died, the cable frayed, or you want to control your PC from the couch, your smartphone touchscreen can act as a fully responsive multi-touch trackpad over local Wi-Fi.

Updated 27 August 2026 · Windows 10 & 11 · Android 5.0+ · Zero cloud account

Direct Answer

Yes. An Android phone can work as a mouse for Windows using a local-network remote-control application. PCDeck turns your phone touchscreen into a multi-touch trackpad and sends cursor movements, clicks, and scroll gestures directly to your Windows PC over local Wi-Fi or a phone mobile hotspot without requiring a cloud account.

Direct Local Setup

PCDeck turns your Android phone into an offline multi-touch trackpad and keyboard. Packets travel straight between devices over your local Wi-Fi router or phone hotspot with no cloud relay.

How smartphone trackpad emulation works

Capacitive smartphone touchscreens provide high-fidelity multi-touch tracking. When you run PCDeck, the Android app captures touch coordinates, applies ballistic curve smoothing and tremor filtering, and transmits lightweight commands over a local TCP WebSocket connection directly to the Windows server on port 8000.

Windows receives the coordinates and synthesizes native mouse events via Win32 input simulation (SendInput and mouse_event). Because all communication occurs over your local network rather than traversing third-party cloud servers, pointer movement feels immediate and fluid on standard local Wi-Fi or phone mobile hotspots.

Step-by-step setup guide

  1. Launch PCDeck on your Windows PC

    Download PCDeck.exe. Run it on your Windows 10 or 11 computer. A clean status window appears displaying your local network IP and a pairing QR code. No installation wizard or background driver is required.

  2. Open the PCDeck app on your Android phone

    Install PCDeck.apk on your phone (Android 5.0 through Android 16+). Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as the PC, or connect your PC to the phone's mobile hotspot.

  3. Pair the two devices

    Tap the Scan QR button in the phone app and scan the QR code on your PC monitor. Alternatively, tap the discovered computer name in the local device list. Connection establishes in under two seconds.

  4. Start navigating immediately

    The phone screen transforms into a trackpad surface. Move your finger across the glass to navigate Windows. Tap the keyboard button in the top bar whenever you need to enter text or use hotkeys.

Wi-Fi vs. Phone Mobile Hotspot

You do not need an active internet connection to use your phone as a mouse. PCDeck works across two network modes:

  • Home or Office Wi-Fi: Both devices connect to your regular router. Packets stay strictly inside your local subnet (192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x) and never touch the outside internet.
  • Phone Mobile Hotspot (Completely Offline): Turn on your Android phone's Personal Hotspot and connect your Windows PC to the phone's Wi-Fi network. PCDeck works flawlessly even in a basement, on a plane, or during a complete home broadband outage.

Network Isolation Tip

If your router has AP Client Isolation enabled (common on public university or hotel Wi-Fi networks that prevent devices from talking to each other), simply switch your phone to Hotspot mode and connect your laptop directly to the phone.
