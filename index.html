Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PCDeck.

Does PCDeck require an active internet connection? +
No! PCDeck operates 100% on your local Wi-Fi network or phone's mobile hotspot. It requires zero internet access, has no cloud accounts, and transmits no telemetry data.
How does direct touchscreen clicking work in Screen Streaming mode? +
When you touch any window, icon, or button on the live screen stream, PCDeck translates your touch coordinates directly into high-precision native Windows Win32 cursor clicks at the exact desktop pixel location.
How do I activate PCDeck Pro features? +
PCDeck Free includes full trackpad gestures, live 30 FPS screen streaming, wireless audio streaming, dual file manager, and keyboard typing. You can activate Pro (60/120 FPS streaming, Turbo multi-stream transfers, and Chroma themes) directly inside the PCDeck app using your lifetime license key (`PCDK-...`).
Can I use PCDeck if my computer monitor is broken or disconnected? +
Yes! PCDeck autostarts silently on Windows boot. Even with zero physical monitors plugged in, your phone automatically connects over local Wi-Fi and provides a full-color 60 FPS touchscreen display so you can access your desktop, launch programs, or safely back up files.
What if my mouse or keyboard suddenly stops working? +
PCDeck serves as an emergency hardware backup. Your smartphone turns into a natural gesture trackpad (with two-finger scrolling, left/right clicks, and drag-and-drop) alongside an on-screen mechanical keyboard, numpad, and typing sync to keep you productive without running to the store for replacement hardware.
How do I listen to PC games and movies in bed or another room? +
PCDeck captures live 48kHz stereo system audio from Windows and streams it directly to your phone. Plug your headphones or wireless earbuds into your phone to enjoy games, YouTube, or Netflix with crystal-clear sound and zero latency from anywhere in the house.
Which Android versions are supported? +
PCDeck is built with modern Android 16 (API 36) backward compatibility and supports all devices running Android 5.0 Lollipop through Android 16+.
