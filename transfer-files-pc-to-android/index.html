How to transfer files between PC and Android without a cable

Moving files between your phone and computer shouldn't require hunting for a USB cable or uploading private documents to cloud servers. Here is how to transfer files wirelessly over local Wi-Fi with zero data usage.

Updated 26 August 2026 · Windows 10 and 11 · Local offline transfer

Local Wi-Fi transfer

If USB cable MTP transfers keep disconnecting or cloud uploads are too slow, PCDeck lets you browse PC files and transfer gigabytes at LAN speeds directly to your phone. Jump to file transfer steps or see common connection problems below.

Common Problems with Other File Transfer Methods

Moving files usually comes with annoying headaches:

  • USB Cables: Cables often disconnect randomly, freeze when copying big video folders, or require missing drivers.
  • Cloud Storage (Drive / OneDrive): Wastes your internet data uploading files to the cloud only to download them again.
  • Bluetooth: Extremely slow — sending a single large video can take 20 minutes or fail halfway through.

How to Transfer Files Wirelessly with PCDeck

PCDeck lets you move photos, videos, and folders directly between your computer and phone over your home Wi-Fi in seconds — no internet or cables needed.

  1. Open PCDeck on your PC and Phone

    Make sure both are connected to the same Wi-Fi (or your phone's hotspot).

  2. Open the File Manager tab

    You will see your computer's folders (Downloads, Desktop, Pictures) directly on your phone screen.

  3. Tap to download or send

    Choose any files to transfer instantly. If your Wi-Fi hiccups, it picks up right where it left off.
