[FULL DESCRIPTION] (Copy text between dashed lines)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Turn your Android phone into an ultra-low-latency wireless trackpad, mechanical keyboard, desktop screen mirror, and high-speed local file transfer hub for Windows 10 and 11.

PCDeck connects directly over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot with zero cloud relay, zero telemetry, and zero account requirements. Experience desktop control that feels responsive, tactile, and natural.


KEY FEATURES:

1. PRECISION MULTI-TOUCH TRACKPAD
- Smooth cursor navigation with ballistic acceleration and tremor filtering.
- 1-Finger Tap: Instant left click.
- 2-Finger Tap: Right click context menu.
- 1-Finger Long Press: Tactile drag-and-drop lock.
- Dedicated Scroll Strip & 2-Finger Kinetic Scrolling: Calibrated inertia that matches native mobile physics.

2. FULL VIRTUAL KEYBOARD & NUMPAD
- Complete desktop layout with Function keys (F1–F12), Esc, Tab, Ctrl, Alt, and Windows key.
- Dedicated Numpad for rapid data entry and spreadsheet navigation.
- One-tap common shortcuts: Copy (Ctrl+C), Paste (Ctrl+V), Task Switcher (Alt+Tab), Show Desktop (Win+D), and screenshot triggers.
- Full media deck: Play/Pause, Next, Previous, Volume Up/Down, and Mute.
- Unicode typing synchronization for fast text input.

3. ZERO-LAG PC SCREEN STREAMING & DIRECT TOUCH
- Low-latency binary desktop streaming directly to your phone screen.
- 1:1 Direct Physical Tracking: Dragging content moves your Windows screen at exact 1:1 finger velocity.
- Kinetic momentum and fling physics with instant tap-to-stop responsiveness.
- Adaptive streaming quality (30/60 FPS) with dynamic resolution scaling.

4. LIVE PC AUDIO STREAMING
- Stream your PC stereo audio output directly to your phone.
- Listen privately through headphones or wireless earbuds over Wi-Fi without needing long audio cables.

5. HIGH-SPEED LOCAL FILE TRANSFER
- Transfer documents, videos, photos, and archives between your phone and PC at full Wi-Fi speeds.
- Direct chunked streaming with automatic download resume support.
- Clean dedicated folders: Downloads/PCDeck on Android and Downloads/PCDeck_Transfers on Windows.


100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY FOCUSED:
- No user accounts, logins, or registration required.
- No cloud servers, analytics, or background telemetry.
- Direct peer-to-peer connection over your local network (LAN or phone hotspot).


QUICK 3-STEP SETUP:
1. Download and launch the free PCDeck Windows companion app on your PC.
2. Ensure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network or Mobile Hotspot.
3. Open PCDeck on Android and scan the on-screen pairing QR code or enter your local PC IP.


SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS:
- Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit).
- Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or newer.
- Shared local Wi-Fi network or Mobile Hotspot.

          HOW TO UPLOAD TO GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE (FROM PHONE OR PC)
================================================================================

1. GO TO GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE:
   - Visit: https://play.google.com/console
   - Log in with your registered Google Play Developer Account.

2. CREATE APP (OR SELECT EXISTING APP):
   - Click "Create app" (top right).
   - App name: PCDeck: WiFi Mouse & Trackpad
   - Default language: English (United States)
   - App or game: App
   - Free or paid: Free
   - Accept declarations and click "Create app".

3. APP CONTENT & STORE PRESENCE:
   - In left menu, navigate to "Store presence" -> "Main store listing".
   - Paste the App title, Short description, and Full description from above.
   - Upload App Icon (512x512 PNG, provided as icon-512.png in PCDeck).
   - Upload Feature Graphic (1024x500 PNG).
   - Upload phone screenshots (minimum 2 screenshots).

4. UPLOAD APP BUNDLE (.AAB):
   - In left menu, go to "Release" -> "Production" (or "Internal testing" / "Closed testing" for your first release).
   - Click "Create new release".
   - Under "App bundles", click "Upload" and choose "PCDeck.aab" (located in your phone's Download folder).
   - Release name: 1.0.0 (1)
   - Release notes: Initial release of PCDeck - Zero-lag WiFi trackpad, keyboard, screen mirror & file transfer.
   - Click "Next" -> "Save" -> "Review release".

5. SUBMIT FOR REVIEW:
   - Complete any remaining Store declaration checklists (Data Safety, Privacy Policy, Target Audience).
   - Click "Start rollout to Production" to submit for Google Play review!
