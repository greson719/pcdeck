How to Connect PC to Another PC Using Wi-Fi (Without Internet or Cables)

Learn how to wirelessly connect two computers over local Wi-Fi to control a desktop from a laptop, stream screens at up to 60 FPS, bypass Windows Home Remote Desktop restrictions, and transfer gigabytes of files without cloud accounts or USB drives.

Updated September 2026 · 100% Peer-to-Peer · Works on Windows Home & Pro · Zero Client Install

The 30-Second Fast Method

To connect and control a PC from another PC or laptop over Wi-Fi without cables or internet:

  1. Connect both computers to the same local Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot.
  2. Launch PCDeck on the Host PC (it starts instantly on port 8000).
  3. On the Client PC/laptop, open Chrome, Edge, or Firefox and navigate to http://<Host-IP>:8000.
  4. Click Screen to stream the host desktop, control mouse and keyboard, stream PC audio, and drag-and-drop transfer files.

Why Connect Two PCs Wirelessly Over Wi-Fi?

There are four common scenarios where connecting two computers over a local Wi-Fi network is far superior to physical KVM cables, secondary monitors, or slow cloud software:

  • Controlling a Heavy Gaming Desktop from a Lightweight Laptop: Access your multi-core GPU desktop from the couch, bed, or balcony without sitting at your desk.
  • Managing a Headless Mini-PC or Home Server: Control a living room media server, NAS, or Intel NUC without keeping a monitor, keyboard, or mouse plugged into it.
  • Emergency Display & Mouse Replacement: When a desktop monitor breaks, a display cable disconnects, or a mouse dies, your laptop functions as an emergency monitor and input console.
  • High-Speed File Sharing Without USB Sticks: Transfer 4K videos, ISO files, and folders between two computers at maximum 5 GHz Wi-Fi speeds without cloud upload delays.

Step-by-Step Guide: Connect PC to PC Over Local Wi-Fi

Step 1: Connect Both PCs to the Same Wi-Fi Network

Verify that both computers are connected to the same wireless router network. If you are away from home, camping, or on a train without a router, enable the Mobile Hotspot feature on your smartphone or host laptop and connect both PCs to it. No internet connection is required.

Step 2: Start PCDeck on the Host Computer

Download and run PCDeck.exe on the host computer you wish to control. PCDeck starts as a lightweight, non-intrusive local server on port 8000.

The status dashboard will immediately display your host PC's local IP address (for example, http://192.168.1.105:8000).

Step 3: Open the Client Laptop / Secondary PC Browser

You do not need to install any program, extension, or driver on your second computer.

  1. Open Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari on your laptop.
  2. In the address bar, type the host PC's IP address: http://192.168.1.105:8000 and press Enter.
  3. The instant PCDeck Web Controller interface loads immediately.

Step 4: Stream Desktop Screen & Remote Control

Once connected, click the Screen tab in the navigation dock:

  • Live 60 FPS Desktop Stream: View the host computer's monitor session in real-time with sub-30ms local latency.
  • Trackpad & Mouse Interaction: Click, drag, and double-click using your laptop's trackpad or physical mouse.
  • Keyboard Chords: Type into remote applications, execute shortcuts (Win+D, Alt+Tab, Ctrl+C), and open Task Manager.
  • Stereo Audio Pass-Through: Listen to PC sound, videos, and games through your laptop speakers or connected headphones.

Wireless File Transfer Between Both Computers

Transferring multi-gigabyte files between two Windows PCs usually involves creating network SMB shares, fighting Windows Defender permission prompts, or finding a USB flash drive.

With PCDeck active between the two PCs:

  1. Click the Files tab on the client laptop's browser.
  2. Browse folders on the host PC directly.
  3. Drag files from your laptop into the browser window to upload them to the host PC at full Wi-Fi throughput.
  4. Click any file on the host PC to download it directly to your laptop's Downloads folder.

Troubleshooting Local Wi-Fi PC-to-PC Connections

If your second laptop or PC cannot reach the host IP address, check these three common network configurations:

1. Windows Firewall Local Access Prompt

When launching PCDeck for the first time, Windows Defender Firewall may display a security popup asking for network permissions. Ensure you check Private networks (home or work) and click Allow access.

2. Router AP Isolation (Client Isolation)

Some university, hotel, or public Wi-Fi networks enable "AP Isolation", preventing two Wi-Fi devices from talking to each other directly.

Workaround: Turn on your mobile phone's Personal Hotspot, connect both PCs to it, and pair instantly. The transfer stays 100% local and will not consume mobile cellular data.

3. Finding Your Local LAN IP via Command Line

If you want to manually verify your host PC's IP address, open PowerShell or Command Prompt on the host PC and type: 

ipconfig

Look for the IPv4 Address under your active Wi-Fi adapter (e.g., 192.168.1.XX).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this require an active internet connection?

No. PCDeck communicates directly over your local Wi-Fi router or hotspot using local TCP and WebSocket protocols. Your internet connection can be completely disconnected, and the remote desktop control, screen mirroring, and file transfers will function uninterrupted.

Does PCDeck work between Windows 11 Home and Windows 10 Home?

Yes. PCDeck is completely independent of Microsoft's RDP license limitations. You can connect a Windows 11 Home laptop to a Windows 10 Home desktop without purchasing Windows Pro upgrades.

Can I control a PC from a MacBook, Chromebook, or iPad?

Yes! Because the client controller runs entirely in standard HTML5 and WebSockets, you can connect from macOS Safari, Chrome OS, Linux, iPadOS, or Android tablets without installing any client software.

What is the streaming latency over local Wi-Fi?

On a modern 5 GHz Wi-Fi 5 or Wi-Fi 6 router, latency is typically between 15ms and 35ms. PCDeck supports 30 FPS and 60 FPS streaming modes with adaptive JPEG compression for buttery-smooth interaction.

Can I hear sound from the host PC on my laptop?

Yes. PCDeck captures Windows WASAPI loopback stereo audio and streams it to your client browser, allowing you to hear games, media players, and system alerts through your laptop speakers.
