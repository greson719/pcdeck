How to Connect Your iPhone Camera to Windows (30 Seconds)

Connecting your iPhone to your Windows PC takes under 30 seconds and requires no USB cables or App Store downloads:

1

Start PCDeck on Your Windows PC

Download and launch PCDeck from the Microsoft Store or run the portable PCDeck.exe. The desktop app immediately shows an optical pairing QR code and binds to your local Wi-Fi.

2

Scan the QR Code with Your iPhone

Open the normal iOS Camera app on your iPhone and point it at your computer screen. Tap the yellow Safari notification pop-up. Mobile Safari opens your PC's local pairing URL with zero sign-ins.

3

Tap Camera → "Start Camera"

In the PCDeck touch interface on your iPhone, navigate to the Camera tab and tap Start Camera. Tap Allow when iOS asks for camera access. Your phone immediately activates an on-device viewfinder with a green LIVE indicator.

4

Pick "PCDeck Camera" in Your PC Apps

Open Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Discord, OBS Studio, or Slack on Windows. Go to Video Settings and select PCDeck Camera. Your iPhone video streams in real-time with sub-30ms latency.

Frequently Asked Questions: iPhone as Windows Webcam

Do I need to install any app from the Apple App Store?

No. That is PCDeck’s main architectural advantage. When you scan the pairing QR code with your native iPhone camera, iOS opens the web remote inside mobile Safari. WebRTC and HTML5 canvas APIs stream high-resolution video frames directly to your Windows PC over your local Wi-Fi without installing third-party apps or creating an account.

Will my video lag or stutter during Zoom or Google Meet calls?

No. PCDeck uses hardware-accelerated local frame capture and pushes frames directly over your local home network (LAN). Because packets never travel through the public internet, latency is typically under 30 milliseconds—completely imperceptible in video calls and live streaming.

Can I switch between the front selfie camera and the rear 48MP camera?

Yes. The PCDeck camera toolbar features a dedicated lens-switch toggle and camera selector. You can easily switch between the ultra-sharp rear main camera (for studio quality) and the front TrueDepth camera, or toggle the rear LED flashlight torch for fill lighting in dark rooms.

Does PCDeck upload my video or camera stream to the cloud?

Never. PCDeck runs a 100% private, peer-to-peer server directly on your Windows PC. There are no external cloud relays, no cloud servers, and zero telemetry. Your camera stream never leaves your private Wi-Fi network.
