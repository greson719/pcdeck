Control mouse input, send keystrokes, stream live desktop video, listen to PC audio, and transfer files between your PC and phone—all over your local network without accounts or internet dependency.

Architecture: Why local Wi-Fi beats cloud remote desktop

Traditional remote desktop software (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, Chrome Remote Desktop) routes your screen and keyboard input through centralized cloud servers on the internet. This introduces several drawbacks:

  • Internet Latency: Input packets must travel to a remote cloud datacenter and back, causing noticeable pointer lag (60–150 ms).
  • Privacy Concerns: Your screen images and keystrokes leave your premises and traverse commercial infrastructure.
  • Outage Fragility: If your ISP connection drops or the cloud service suffers downtime, you cannot control a PC sitting two feet in front of you.

PCDeck's Local Architecture: All packets travel directly between your phone and PC over your router or hotspot using raw local TCP/UDP sockets. Latency stays under 10–20 ms, and control continues uninterrupted even if your internet connection is unplugged.

Step-by-step connection guide

  1. Download and run PCDeck on your Windows PC

    Launch PCDeck.exe. The window shows your local IP address and a QR code. On first launch, check Private networks on the Windows Firewall prompt.

  2. Install PCDeck on Android

    Download PCDeck.apk on your phone. Grant local network permission and ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

  3. Pair in one tap

    Tap Scan QR on your phone and point your camera at the PC screen, or tap your computer's name in the auto-discovery list.

  4. Switch between tools on the fly

    Use the bottom toolbar on Android to seamlessly transition between Trackpad, Keyboard, Screen Streaming, Audio, and Files without reconnecting.

Connecting when you have no Wi-Fi router (Hotspot Mode)

If you are travelling, working in a hotel room, or experiencing a home power outage, you can pair completely offline:

  1. Turn on Portable Hotspot on your Android phone (Settings → Network & Internet → Hotspot).
  2. Connect your Windows PC to your phone's Wi-Fi hotspot SSID.
  3. Launch PCDeck on both devices. They will detect each other across the hotspot subnet with zero internet data usage.
