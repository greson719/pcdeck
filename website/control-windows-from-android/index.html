How to Control a Windows PC from Your Phone

Turn your phone into a multi-tool for Windows over local Wi-Fi: wireless mouse, keyboard, emergency monitor, audio streamer, and cable-free file manager. Zero cloud accounts required.

Updated September 2026 · Windows 10 & 11 · Works on iPhone & Android · 100% Offline LAN

Direct Answer

Yes, and you don't even need to download an app on your phone. PCDeck runs a standalone server on Windows. By scanning the QR code on your PC monitor, your phone's browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) instantly connects over local Wi-Fi. You get full mouse control, keyboard typing, real-time desktop display viewing, audio streaming, and file transfers with zero cloud relay.

Two Ways to Connect: Browser vs. App

Option 1 · Recommended

Instant Browser (Zero Phone Install)

Works on iPhone, iPad, Android & Mac. You don't have to download anything on your phone.

  1. Download and run PCDeck.exe on your PC (Free).
  2. Scan the on-screen QR code with your phone camera.
  3. Tap the link — your phone browser opens the complete remote controller immediately!
Option 2 · Optional

Dedicated Android App (APK)

For Android users who want a permanent app icon on their home screen with hardware volume control.

  1. Download the lightweight PCDeck.apk (739 KB).
  2. Tap your PC in the auto-discovery list.
  3. Enjoy haptic feedback and instant auto-reconnect.

100% Offline: Works even without an internet connection

PCDeck does not route data through any cloud servers. Everything runs strictly on your local Wi-Fi network. If your home internet is down or you are traveling without a router, simply turn on your phone's Personal Mobile Hotspot, connect your PC to it, and PCDeck will work with full speed and zero internet data usage.
