How to Use Your Phone as a Screen for Windows

Need an emergency second display, want to monitor your PC from another room, or dealing with a broken laptop screen? You don't even need to install an app on your phone. Just scan a QR code and stream your desktop immediately.

Updated September 2026 · iPhone, iPad & Android · 100% Free · No Phone App Needed

Direct Answer

Yes, and you don't even need to download an app on your phone. PCDeck captures your Windows desktop and streams it directly over your local Wi-Fi. By scanning the QR code on your PC, your phone's browser (Safari or Chrome) instantly turns into a responsive desktop monitor that lets you see your open windows and click on icons using your touchscreen.

Why stream your PC screen to your phone?

Using your phone as an auxiliary or emergency PC screen solves real frustrations:

  • Cracked or dark laptop monitor: A dropped laptop with a broken screen still has functioning internals. Put the desktop on your phone or tablet and rescue your files or finish your assignment.
  • Second screen for chat or guides: Keep Discord, Spotify, or a game walkthrough open on your phone screen next to your monitor while you game in full-screen on your PC.
  • Headless media PC in a closet: If you run a Plex server or file server without a dedicated monitor, check on background tasks from your couch instead of carrying a monitor across the house.
  • Watch movies in bed: Watch video files playing on your PC directly on your phone screen with synchronized PC audio streamed to your earbuds.

How touch clicks map to Windows elements

When you look at your PC desktop on your phone screen, you don't need a separate mouse. Tapping on a folder icon or browser button maps directly to that coordinate on Windows. Tapping with one finger performs a left click; holding or tapping with two fingers performs a right click.
