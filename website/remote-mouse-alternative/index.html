Where Remote Mouse Genuinely Excels

To be completely fair, Remote Mouse has been in development for over a decade and offers specialized features that PCDeck does not intend to replicate:

  • Gyroscope Air-Mouse: If you want to stand across the room and physically wave your phone in mid-air like a presenter wand or Nintendo Wii remote, Remote Mouse has built-in gyroscope tracking.
  • Native App Store Polish: Remote Mouse has dedicated native apps with haptic vibration engines and customized icon skins for macOS and Windows media players.
  • Longstanding Community: Remote Mouse is widely recognized and has millions of downloads across global app stores.

The 4 Big Reasons Users Switch to PCDeck

Despite Remote Mouse's popularity, thousands of users seek alternatives every month due to four specific friction points:

1. Zero Full-Screen Ads Interrupting Your Cursor

There is nothing more frustrating than trying to click a button on your computer and being blocked by a 30-second unskippable video ad on your phone screen. Remote Mouse's free tier has become heavily ad-supported. PCDeck has zero advertising, zero tracking scripts, and zero sponsored popups.

2. No Mobile App Download Required

With Remote Mouse, you cannot do anything until you open the App Store or Google Play, wait for an 80MB download, grant device permissions, and accept terms. Remote Mouse is purely an input device (mouse and keyboard). You can also view your desktop monitor directly on your phone screen.

4. Portable, Zero-Install Desktop Binary

Remote Mouse installs system drivers, background autostart services, and registry keys. PCDeck is a clean, portable single executable file. When you close it, nothing remains running in the background.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PCDeck safe to run on Windows?

Yes. PCDeck is 100% offline and operates solely over your private local area network (LAN). It sends zero telemetry to the cloud and requires no user account. You can verify the executable checksum on our official download page.

Does PCDeck work on iPhone as well as Android?

Yes. Because the remote interface is rendered in modern web browsers via WebSockets, it works seamlessly on Apple iOS (Safari), iPadOS, Android (Google Chrome, Firefox), and any device connected to your local Wi-Fi.

What happens if I don't have internet access?

PCDeck does not need the internet. It communicates over your local Wi-Fi router or directly through your phone's mobile hotspot.
