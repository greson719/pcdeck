How to Use a PC Without a Monitor

Your laptop screen cracked, your external monitor went black, or you have a headless PC running in a closet. Here is how to diagnose the issue blindly and stream your desktop directly to your phone screen over Wi-Fi.

Updated September 2026 · Windows 10 & 11 · Works on iPhone, iPad & Android · Emergency Display

Direct Answer

Yes. If your monitor is dead or cracked, you can turn your smartphone or tablet (iPhone, iPad, or Android) into an emergency wireless display. PCDeck streams your full Windows desktop over local Wi-Fi directly to your phone. Simply open the connection in your phone's browser (or the native app) to see and tap on your Windows desktop in real-time with zero cloud delay.

Emergency Fix: Use Your Phone as Your Monitor

You don't need a spare monitor, an HDMI dummy plug, or any phone app downloads:

STEP 3: DONE! See Desktop Live Tap the Screen tab. Your desktop appears live. Tap anywhere on your phone to click!

Step 1: Diagnose if Windows is actually booting

Before assuming the worst, check if the PC itself is alive behind the dark screen:

  • Check the Caps Lock key: Press the Caps Lock key on your keyboard. If its small indicator LED turns on and off, Windows has booted and the motherboard and OS are responsive.
  • Listen for the Windows chime or fan activity: Fans spinning up and quiet Windows startup sounds confirm Windows has reached the sign-in screen.
  • Blind login trick: Turn on the PC, wait 45 seconds for Windows to reach the lock screen. Press Spacebar or Enter to dismiss the lock screen image, type your Windows PIN or password, and hit Enter.

Step 2: Windows keyboard shortcuts to recover video

Sometimes Windows simply routed the video output to the wrong display port or got stuck in sleep mode:

Windows shortcuts for display recovery
Keyboard ShortcutWhat It Does
Win + Ctrl + Shift + BRestarts your graphics driver. The screen flashes, your PC beeps, and the display subsystem resets. Solves black screens caused by GPU driver crashes.
Win + P, then Down Arrow, then EnterCycles Project display modes. If Windows thinks it is on an unplugged second monitor, this switches back to Duplicate or Primary display.
Win + Ctrl + EnterTurns on Windows Narrator. Narrator speaks whatever is focused on screen through your speakers or headphones, giving you audio cues even with zero visual output.

How PCDeck lets you use a PC without any physical screen

When you have a desktop machine in a closet (headless server, plex media player) or a laptop with a smashed LCD panel, PCDeck completely removes the need to plug in a monitor:

  • Start with Windows automatically: Place a shortcut to PCDeck in your Windows Startup folder (press Win+R, type shell:startup, paste PCDeck). Every time the PC powers on, PCDeck starts broadcasting screen streaming immediately.
  • Real-time touch interaction: Unlike passive screen mirroring, PCDeck maps your finger taps to native Windows mouse clicks. Tapping a window close button or taskbar icon on your phone clicks that exact button on Windows.
  • Stream PC audio to phone: If the PC has no speakers attached, PCDeck streams clear 48 kHz stereo audio directly into your phone headphones.
  • 100% offline local Wi-Fi: Works even if your internet service is down or you connect directly via your phone's mobile hotspot.
