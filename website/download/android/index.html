How to Install PCDeck APK

PCDeck is distributed directly as an ultra-lightweight APK (1.5 MB) with zero tracking, zero ads, and zero account requirements.

STEP 1

Tap Downloaded APK

Open your phone's notification bar or Downloads folder and tap PCDeck.apk to begin installation.

STEP 2

Allow Unknown Sources

If Android prompts "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and allow permission for Chrome or your file manager. If Play Protect appears, select Install anyway.

STEP 3

Instant LAN Discovery

Ensure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi. Open PCDeck on Android → it automatically finds your PC server or lets you scan the QR code.
