How to Use Your Phone as a Mouse for Windows

Whether your physical mouse died or you want to pause movies from your couch, you don't even need to install an app on your phone. Just scan a QR code and your phone screen instantly becomes a wireless trackpad.

Updated September 2026 · Works on iPhone, Android & iPad · 100% Free · No App Download on Phone

Direct Answer

Yes, and you don't even need to download an app on your phone. PCDeck runs a lightweight local server on your Windows PC. When you open it, a QR code appears on your monitor. Simply point your iPhone or Android camera at the screen, tap the link, and your mobile web browser instantly becomes a responsive multi-touch trackpad with left-click, two-finger right-click, and smooth scrolling over your local Wi-Fi.

How it works (In Simple Terms)

You don't need any special hardware, cables, or Bluetooth adapters. PCDeck connects your computer and phone over your home Wi-Fi or mobile phone hotspot.

When you glide your finger across your phone screen, it sends high-speed touch commands directly across your local network. Your Windows cursor moves instantly with zero perceptible delay — exactly like a built-in laptop trackpad.

Wi-Fi vs. Phone Mobile Hotspot

You do not need an active internet connection to use your phone as a mouse. PCDeck works across two network modes:

  • Home or Office Wi-Fi: Both devices connect to your regular router. Packets stay strictly inside your local subnet (192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x) and never touch the outside internet.
  • Phone Mobile Hotspot (Completely Offline): Turn on your Android phone's Personal Hotspot and connect your Windows PC to the phone's Wi-Fi network. PCDeck works flawlessly even in a basement, on a plane, or during a complete home broadband outage.

Network Isolation Tip

If your router has AP Client Isolation enabled (common on public university or hotel Wi-Fi networks that prevent devices from talking to each other), simply switch your phone to Hotspot mode and connect your laptop directly to the phone.
