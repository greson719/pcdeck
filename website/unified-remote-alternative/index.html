The 4 Reasons Users Switch from Unified Remote to PCDeck

1. Zero Background Services or Startup Daemons

Unified Remote installs a background Windows service that runs continuously in Task Manager, uses system memory, and auto-starts on boot. PCDeck is a portable single executable file. When you close it, all processes terminate immediately. Nothing is left running.

2. Instant QR Code Connection Without App Stores

Connecting a phone to Unified Remote requires searching app stores, downloading the app, waiting for Wi-Fi discovery, and sometimes typing in manual IP addresses. With PCDeck, simply point your phone's camera at the on-screen QR code and control your PC immediately in Safari or Chrome.

3. Wireless PC Audio Streaming to Phone Headphones

Unified Remote does not stream audio. PCDeck features native WASAPI audio capture, allowing you to stream your computer's sound directly to your phone earbuds with low latency—ideal for watching movies or videos privately.

4. Completely Free with Zero Paywalls

Unified Remote locks screen mirroring, quick switch actions, and custom remotes behind a paid license. PCDeck offers precision trackpad, keyboard, numpad, audio streamer, and screen viewer 100% free with zero advertisements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PCDeck require administrator privileges to install?

No. PCDeck is a portable executable that runs entirely in user space. It does not install kernel drivers or background services.

Can I control my PC if my Wi-Fi has client isolation?

Yes. If your Wi-Fi router blocks device-to-device communication, simply turn on your phone's personal hotspot and connect your PC to it. PCDeck pairs instantly.
