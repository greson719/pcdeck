Where Unified Remote Still Wins

Unified Remote has been actively maintained for over a decade and remains the unmatched king in several specialized scenarios:

  • 100+ Dedicated App Remotes: If you specifically want individual custom button dashboards designed for obscure media players (BSPlayer, MediaMonkey, foobar2000, Boxee), Unified Remote has custom community remotes for nearly everything.
  • Lua Scripting Engine: If you are a developer or home automation enthusiast who wants to write custom remote controls with custom buttons and Python/Lua callbacks, Unified Remote's remote creation toolkit is mature.
  • Bluetooth RF Fallback: If you are in a location with strictly no Wi-Fi router and cannot enable a phone mobile hotspot, Unified Remote can connect over classic Bluetooth RF.

Why Users Switch from Unified Remote to PCDeck

For the vast majority of users who simply want a fast, reliable wireless trackpad, keyboard, media remote, and audio streamer, Unified Remote feels overly heavy and dated:

1. Zero Setup Overhead & No Background Services

Unified Remote installs a Windows service that runs constantly in task manager, listens on multiple ports, and frequently trips up Windows Firewall. PCDeck requires zero installation. Double-click the `.exe`, and it starts. When you close the window, it stops. There are no background services left running on your PC.

2. Instant QR Code Connection (No App Stores)

Connecting a new phone to Unified Remote means searching Google Play or the iOS App Store, downloading the client app, waiting for Wi-Fi discovery, and sometimes typing in IP addresses and passwords. With PCDeck, anyone in the room can scan the on-screen QR code with their default camera app and instantly control the PC in Safari or Chrome.

3. Wireless PC Audio Streaming Included Free

One of the most common user requests for PC remotes is listening to the computer's sound through phone headphones while sitting on the couch or in bed. Unified Remote does not support PC audio capture. PCDeck features native WASAPI Loopback streaming, routing 48 kHz stereo audio directly to your phone.

4. 100% Free with No Ads or Paywalls

Unified Remote locks screen mirroring, quick switch actions, and custom controls behind a paid license. PCDeck offers trackpad, keyboard, numpad, audio streamer, and screen viewer 100% free with zero advertisements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use PCDeck on public or restricted Wi-Fi?

Yes. If client isolation is enabled on a shared Wi-Fi router (common in universities or hotels), simply turn on your phone's personal mobile hotspot and connect your PC to it. PCDeck will pair instantly over the hotspot.

Does PCDeck support media key shortcuts?

Yes. PCDeck includes dedicated shortcuts for play/pause, volume up/down, mute, next/previous track, browser tab switching, and Windows window management.

Is an internet connection required?

No. PCDeck functions entirely within your local area network without transmitting any data over the public internet.
