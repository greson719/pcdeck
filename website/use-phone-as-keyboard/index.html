Why turn your phone into a PC keyboard?

Having a phone-based keyboard available is an immediate lifesaver in several everyday situations:

  • Hardware keyboard failure: Liquid spills, jammed keys, or a broken laptop ribbon cable leave you unable to type your Windows login PIN or password.
  • Laptops without numeric keypads: Modern ultrabooks omit the 10-key numpad. PCDeck gives you a dedicated numpad on your phone for Excel spreadsheets, accounting software, and Blender hotkeys.
  • Media center and couch PC control: Type search queries into Netflix, YouTube, or browser address bars on a TV-connected PC without lugging a clunky physical keyboard across the room.
  • Voice-to-text dictation: Use your phone's voice recognition (Gboard, Siri, Samsung Voice) to dictate long emails, essays, or notes directly into Word, Notepad, or your browser on Windows.
  • Never blocks your PC monitor: Windows' built-in On-Screen Keyboard covers up 40% of your monitor. Using your phone as a second screen keyboard keeps your PC monitor completely unobstructed.

Windows Security Note

Due to Windows operating system security isolation (Secure Attention Sequence / SAS), user-mode software cannot inject Ctrl+Alt+Del. To open the Windows Task Manager directly without pressing those keys, use the shortcut Ctrl+Shift+Esc.
