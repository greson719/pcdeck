How to Use an Android Phone as a Screen for Windows

Whether your laptop screen cracked, your external monitor lost its video signal, or you need a portable status display, you can stream your live Windows desktop to an Android phone over local Wi-Fi.

Updated 27 August 2026 · Windows 10 & 11 · 30 & 60 FPS Streaming · Local Wi-Fi

Local Desktop Streaming

PCDeck captures your Windows desktop and streams compressed frames directly to your phone screen over local Wi-Fi. You can view the display and interact with Windows using direct touch taps.

How PCDeck screen streaming works

Unlike cloud remote desktop services that upload video feeds to third-party data centers, PCDeck operates entirely on your Local Area Network (LAN):

  1. Direct Desktop Frame Capture

    On Windows, PCDeck uses native desktop duplication APIs to capture screen frames with minimal CPU overhead.

  2. Real-Time Local Streaming

    Frames are compressed and streamed over high-speed local TCP/UDP sockets directly to the phone's IP address.

  3. Hardware Accelerated Decoding on Phone

    The Android app decodes and renders the video stream using the phone's GPU hardware, keeping battery consumption low and latency crisp.

  4. Direct Touch-to-Click Mapping

    Tapping any button or icon on your phone translates to an instantaneous mouse click at the exact corresponding pixel on Windows.

Primary use cases

Using your phone as a PC screen solves critical problems that physical monitors cannot:

  • Emergency Broken Monitor Rescue: When a laptop display is shattered or an external monitor dies, your PC is still running. PCDeck lets you see what is on screen to save open documents, close running processes, or back up important files.
  • Headless PC Management: For mini-PCs, home servers, or living room media rigs without dedicated monitors, connect with your phone to perform quick maintenance, driver updates, or reboots.
  • Mobile System Monitoring: Place your phone on a desk stand next to your keyboard to monitor CPU temps, download progress, Discord chats, or music playback without cluttering your main workspace.

Important technical expectations

To set honest expectations:

  • Streaming vs. Physical Monitor: PCDeck streams the active Windows desktop image. It is an optimized network video feed with touch interaction, designed for utility, emergency recovery, and control—not a zero-latency hardware DisplayPort replacement for competitive eSports gaming.
  • Resolution & Scaling: Because smartphone screens are compact, PCDeck allows pinch-to-zoom and pan navigation so you can clearly read small 1080p or 4K system fonts and dialog boxes.
  • Network Performance: On standard 5 GHz Wi-Fi or phone mobile hotspot, latency is typically between 15–30 ms.

Emergency Recommendation

Set PCDeck to Start with Windows in its settings panel. If your physical screen ever dies unexpectedly in the future, PCDeck will launch automatically on boot, allowing your phone to connect and view the desktop without needing a working monitor to click anything.
