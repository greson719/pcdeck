Frequently Asked Questions

Does PCDeck require administrator privileges to install?

No. PCDeck is a portable executable that runs entirely in user space. It does not install kernel drivers or background services.

Can I control my PC if my Wi-Fi has client isolation?

Yes. If your Wi-Fi router blocks device-to-device communication, simply turn on your phone's personal hotspot and connect your PC to it. PCDeck pairs instantly.
