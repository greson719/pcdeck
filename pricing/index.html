Frequently Asked Questions

Is PCDeck Core really free?

Yes. Every core feature—trackpad gestures, virtual keyboard, 30 FPS screen mirroring, PC audio streaming, phone webcam/mic, and file transfers—is 100% free with no advertisements, tracking, or mandatory registrations.

Is PCDeck Pro a subscription?

No. PCDeck Pro is a single one-time payment of $3.99. You own it forever. There are no monthly fees, no yearly renewals, and no unexpected charges.

How do I activate Pro?

After completing checkout via Dodo Payments, you receive your lifetime activation key which can be entered directly inside the PCDeck app to unlock 60/120 FPS and advanced features instantly.

What payment methods are supported?

Dodo Payments securely supports credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized payment options across 100+ countries.
